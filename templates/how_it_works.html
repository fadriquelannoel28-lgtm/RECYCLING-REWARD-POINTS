How EcoCoins Works

Recycle in 5 easy steps and earn rewards instantly.

1
Collect

Separate plastic bottles, aluminum cans, and other recyclables at home.

2
Scan QR

Present your personal resident QR code to the station scanner.

3
Insert

Drop the recyclable into the deposit slot for sensor detection.

4
Earn Points

The system calculates points based on material type and quantity.

5
Redeem

Use your accumulated EcoPoints to claim discount vouchers and gifts.

Our smart collection station reads your QR code, detects the materials using sensors, and records the transaction directly to the central system without manual paperwork.
