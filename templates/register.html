EcoCoins Terms and Conditions
Barangay Automated Recycling Reward Points System

By creating a resident account on EcoCoins, you agree to the following terms governing your participation in our Barangay recycling and reward program:

1. Resident Eligibility & Barangay Assignment

EcoCoins resident accounts are intended for residents of participating barangays. You must select your true Province, City/Municipality, and Barangay during account verification. Deposits and verification confirmations are handled exclusively by your assigned Barangay Collection Hub staff.

2. Identity Verification & Biometric Privacy

To prevent duplicate accounts and ensure fair reward distribution, residents are required to submit accurate personal details, a valid government/barangay ID photo, and a live facial recognition scan. Your facial descriptor and ID image are used strictly for identity verification, duplicate-account prevention, and secure sign-in within EcoCoins.

3. One-Account-Per-Resident Policy

Each resident may maintain only one verified EcoCoins account. Duplicate verification requests matching an already-verified resident's facial biometrics will be automatically rejected.

4. Recyclable Deposits & EcoPoints

EcoPoints are awarded only after Barangay Staff inspect and record eligible recyclable materials at your assigned station using your verified Resident QR Code. Point values and accepted materials are determined by Barangay EcoCoins administration and may be updated as needed.

5. Reward Redemptions & Account Conduct

Redeemed reward vouchers must be claimed in person at your Barangay Collection Hub. Providing false information, attempting to manipulate point balances, or using another resident's identity or QR code may result in account suspension or deactivation.
